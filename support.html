FAQ

Can ShieldCall answer my cellular calls?

No. iOS does not let a third-party app intercept or auto-answer a normal cell call. The simulator on the phone is a typed conversation. It is labeled SIMULATED. Answering a real call would require your carrier to forward that call to a number you control. That gateway is not part of version 0.1.0.

Do I need the Windows or PC server?

No, for the simulator, history, evidence, block list, and settings. Those stay on the iPhone. You need the self-hosted server only if you want a second copy on your computer, the admin console, or a later live-call path. Leave More, Server sync, empty if you do not want that.

Why does every practice call say SIMULATED?

Because it is not a call that reached your phone from a carrier. The label stays on the row, including in exports. It is there so a practice session cannot be mistaken for a real incident.

What does the assistant refuse to do?

It will not provide a card number, bank account, routing number, Social Security number, or tax id. It will not agree to a purchase or a contract. It will not invent an accounts-payable department or other business facts you did not store. If asked whether it is human, it says it is an automated assistant. The do-not-call closing line, when that option is on, asks the caller to stop marketing to the number. That request is not confirmation that they complied.

How do I block a number?

In More, Carrier setup, save a block rule, then tap the button that asks iOS to reload the Call Directory extension. After that, enable ShieldCall under Settings, Phone, Call Blocking & Identification. The app cannot flip that switch for you. Blocking on the handset still does not answer the call.

Are the forwarding codes safe to dial?

Treat them as unverified examples. They are commonly published carrier codes, not a test of your plan. A wrong forwarding code can send your calls somewhere you did not intend. Read your carrier’s current instructions before you use one, and cancel it if normal calls stop arriving.

How do I export or delete data?

The Evidence tab can share CSV or JSON for a call. More, Privacy and security, deletes call data on the phone after you type DELETE. Retention days are on that same screen. Audio days are stored for a later version. This version does not record audio.
